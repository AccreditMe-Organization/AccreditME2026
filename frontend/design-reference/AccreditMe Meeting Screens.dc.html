Meetings

The meetings list, the Meetings panel on committee and unit records, Schedule meeting with its repeat step, the meeting record page through every stage, attendance, minutes, review and approval, action items (Task Screens, unchanged), series, and cancel. Behaviour questions are listed for the product owner, not decided. Tweaks: the meeting page's stage, and who is looking at it. Hijri dates are Umm al-Qura, anchored on 23 Sep 2026 = 12 Rabiʻ al-Thani 1448. Every example date falls inside that month, so each conversion is checkable. Round 3 brings in the product's stage and move names, review by a date with the Chair approving, action items that become tasks on approval, outside guests, votes, the without-quorum mark, printable minutes, repeat patterns, rescheduling, who works each stage, the list rules, the file viewer and open actions from the last meeting. New drawings are in 12 (review and minutes), 13 (print) and 14 (phone); what changed and the new questions are in 15. Tweaks now run Planned through Cancelled, seen by the Secretary, the Chair, a present member, an absent member or a guest.
