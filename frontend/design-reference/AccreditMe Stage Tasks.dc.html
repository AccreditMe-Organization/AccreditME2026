Stage tasks

A stage carries task definitions; every time a record enters the stage they become real tasks, and mandatory ones block moving forward. Example: Incident Management, stage “Investigating”, deadline 40 working hours. Tweaks switch the stage row between three tasks, empty, no deadline, and a refused deadline.
