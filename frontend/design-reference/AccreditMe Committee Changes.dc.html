Committee changes

A committee is edited directly only in Formation, read-only in Terms Review, changed through a change request with its own approval once Active or Suspended, and closed to change from Dissolution Pending. Members stay outside all of this. Stage names are tenant data and render as data. Example: Infection Prevention & Control Committee (QMC-014), Change request 3 by Nora Al-Otaibi, reviewed by A. Al-Harbi.
